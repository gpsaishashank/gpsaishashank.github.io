Hi there! I'm Sai Shashank. I'm a Ph.D. student at University of Alberta, advised by [Levi Lelis](https://levilelis.github.io/). Previously, I received my Bachelors and Masters in Electrical Engineering from Indian Institute of Technology, Madras. During my Masters, I was fortunate to be advised by [Balaraman Ravindran](https://wsai.iitm.ac.in/~ravi/). 

My research interests are reinforcement learning and neural program synthesis. I focus on studying and understanding how to learn behavioral representations (e.g., macro-actions, options) that are general and transferrable across different objectives. I believe that combining techniques of reinforcement learning and program synthesis could help us learn representations that are modular, composable, and reusable.
